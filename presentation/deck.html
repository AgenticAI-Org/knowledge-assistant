Why "chat with your PDF" isn't enough

Where the baseline breaksWhat this does instead

One retrieval pass. Pure vector search paraphrases well but blurs exact terms — a form code, a filename, a policy named verbatim.

Two passes, fused. Vector and BM25 run on every query, merge by Reciprocal Rank Fusion, then get reranked by a local cross-encoder.

No memory. "What about carry-forward?" retrieves noise, because nothing in that string says leave.

Condense, then retrieve. Each follow-up is rewritten into a standalone query against recent turns before retrieval runs.

Fills gaps by inventing. Asked about a sabbatical policy that doesn't exist, it writes one — and names sources it never read.

Refuses, and cites what it read. A two-stage guardrail declines on weak context; citations come from the chunks in the prompt.

Reciprocal Rank Fusion

Merges the two ranked lists by rank position rather than by score. Cosine similarity and BM25 weights live on incomparable scales, so blending them numerically would mean inventing a normalization — RRF sidesteps the question entirely.
